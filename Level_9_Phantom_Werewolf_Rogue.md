## Backstory & Roleplay Profile: *The Grimshaw Inheritance*

### The Origin: Blood Will Tell
You spent ten years running from **Grimshaw Hall**—a crumbling, mist-choked estate on the edge of the dark woods (*Grimshaw* literally meaning "haunted grove") where the villagers still hang wolfsbane over their doors and cross themselves when your family name is spoken. As a teenager, you thought your father's iron-barred cellar, his "hunting trips" during the autumn moon, and your mother's mysterious death were just the eccentricities of a rotting Gothic bloodline. So you became "Hex"—a rogue, lockpick, cat burglar, and graveside antiquarian in the city—anything to stay behind cobblestone walls and away from the pines.

Six months ago, a telegram arrived: *Your brother Ben was found torn to pieces in the fog. Come home.*

You shouldn't have gone back. The night of the funeral, the autumn moon broke through the clouds, and you learned the truth in the cellar: the beast wasn't hunting your family—**it *was* your family**. Your father, half-mad and monstrous, tried to "pass the mantle" to you before silver-armed hunters burned the manor down. You survived the slaughter, throat torn and veins burning with the generational curse, having had to put down your own flesh and blood in the ashes.

Now, whenever the adrenaline spikes or the moon rises, your jaw lengthens, coarse black fur splits your knuckles, and the **Phantom spirits** of your dead family—your mangled brother Ben, your cynical father, and generations of cursed Grimshaw ancestors—crowd over your shoulder, offering unsolicited advice and tearing into the souls of anyone you cut down.

### Personality: Gallows Humor & Hyper-Vigilant Paranoia
Despite having a 24 Passive Perception when shifted, your default state is a twitchy, sleep-deprived survivor who copes with body horror through **deadpan gallows humor**:
- **Silver Paranoia:** At every tavern or aristocratic dinner, you discreetly test the cutlery against your thumb, sit with your back to the wall, count the windows, and casually ask if the soup has garlic or wolfsbane *"purely for culinary curiosity."*
- **Bickering with the Invisible:** Because of your Phantom **Whispers of the Dead**, you occasionally mutter dry comebacks under your breath to your dead brother Ben or your ancestors—who appear to you exactly as they looked the night they died (like Jack in *An American Werewolf in London*).
- **Roleplay Traits (Haunted One):**
  - **Personality Trait 1:** *"I crack bleak, inappropriate jokes when the fog rolls in—if I don't laugh at the fact that my fingernails turn into talons when I'm stressed, I'll start screaming."*
  - **Personality Trait 2:** *"I never enter a room without clocking every exit, lock, chimney, and whoever is holding silver."*
  - **Ideal:** *"Leash the Beast. The bloodline made me a monster, sure—but I still get to choose whose throat I point it at."*
  - **Bond:** *"My dead brother's pocket watch (my primary Soul Trinket). His ghost won't shut up until I figure out who—or what—originally cursed the Grimshaw bloodline."*
  - **Flaw:** *"Paranoid compulsion: I instinctively sniff the air around strangers, flinch at church bells or silver coins, and hoard morbid mementos from the dead."*

### Starting Soul Trinkets (Tokens of the Departed Flavor)
At the start of a session (or as you harvest them in combat), your up-to-4 **Soul Trinkets** can be roleplayed as heirloom horrors:
1. **Ben's Crushed Pocket Watch:** Stopped at 11:47 PM, stained with dried moor-mud and blood. *(Whispers warnings in your brother's sarcastic voice).*
2. **Father's Grimshaw Signet Ring:** Deeply gouged by four claw marks across the family crest.
3. **A Tarnished Silver Musket Ball:** Dug out of your own shoulder after the burning of Grimshaw Hall; wrapped in leather so it doesn't touch your skin.
4. **A Freshly Harvested Trophy:** A tooth, locket, or bone shard snatched via your **Reaction** from the first monster or cultist that dies within 30 feet of you tonight.

## How "The Werewolf" Works in Combat (Piercer Bite + Slasher Claws!)

Because your rolled **16 DEX** (+2 Racial = 18) allowed you to take **BOTH** the **Piercer (+1 DEX)** and **Slasher (+1 DEX)** half-feats at Levels 4 and 8, you have maxed **20 DEX** alongside the ultimate hybrid predator toolkit:
1. **"Rending Fangs" (Rapier — `1d8` Piercing, Finesse):** Triggers **Piercer**! Once per turn you can **reroll one piercing damage die** (including your `5d6` Sneak Attack dice, since Sneak Attack takes on the weapon's damage type!), and on a Critical Hit you roll **an extra damage die**.
2. **"Hamstringing Talons" (Scimitar / Slashing Claw — `1d6` Slashing, Finesse, Light):** Triggers **Slasher**! Once per turn when you hit a creature with a slashing attack, you **reduce its speed by 10 feet** until the start of your next turn (so victims can't outrun you!), and on a Critical Hit the victim has **Disadvantage on all attack rolls** until the start of your next turn!
3. **"Thrown Debris / Lunging Bite" (Dagger / Whip — `1d4` Piercing or Slashing, Finesse):** Gives you reach or ranged options.

## Attack & Damage Reference

### 1. Primary Bite Attack: Rending Fangs (Rapier — Piercing + Piercer Feat)
- **Attack Bonus:** **+9 to hit** *(+5 DEX, +4 Prof)* — almost always at **Advantage** via *Steady Aim* or *Hiding*.
- **Standard Hit:** `1d8 + 5` piercing + `5d6` Sneak Attack *(Avg: **27 damage**, plus reroll lowest die via Piercer)*.
- **Critical Hit:** `3d8 + 5` piercing + `10d6` Sneak Attack *(Avg: **53.5 damage**)*.
- **Wails from the Grave (Splash Damage):** Immediately after hitting with Sneak Attack, force a second creature within 30 feet of the victim to take **`3d6` Necrotic damage** *(Avg: **10.5 necrotic**, no save, no attack roll!)*.

### 2. Primary Claw Attack: Hamstringing Talons (Scimitar / Slashing Rapier — Slashing + Slasher Feat)
- **Attack Bonus:** **+9 to hit** *(+5 DEX, +4 Prof)*.
- **Standard Hit:** `1d6 + 5` slashing *(or `1d8 + 5` if using a slashing-flavored Rapier)* + `5d6` Sneak Attack + **Target's Speed is reduced by 10 ft.**!
- **Critical Hit:** `2d6 + 5` slashing + `10d6` Sneak Attack + **Target has Disadvantage on ALL attack rolls** until your next turn!
- **Off-Hand Bonus Action Swipe (Pair Shortsword [Piercing] + Scimitar [Slashing]):** Hit with your Main-Hand Piercing Bite (`1d6+5` + `5d6` Sneak Attack + **Piercer reroll**) AND follow up with an Off-Hand Slashing Claw (`1d6` + **-10 ft. enemy speed via Slasher**) in the exact same turn!

### 3. Opportunity Attack Lunge (Reaction)
- **Reaction:** When a hostile creature moves out of your reach, you make a melee attack with your Bite or Claws.
- **Sneak Attack on Another Turn:** Because Sneak Attack is once per *turn* (not per round), if an ally is within 5 feet of the target or you have advantage on your reaction attack, you deal full **`5d6` Sneak Attack** on the reaction!

## Complete Feature Breakdown (Levels 1–9)

### Racial Traits: Wildhunt Shifter (*Monsters of the Multiverse*)
- **Darkvision (60 ft.):** You see in dim light and darkness like a nocturnal predator.
- **Bestial Instincts:** Proficiency in *Survival* (or *Acrobatics/Athletics/Intimidation*).
- **Shifting (Bonus Action, 4 / Long Rest):**
  - Lasts for **1 minute**.
  - You immediately gain **13 Temporary Hit Points** (`PB 4 + Level 9`).
  - **Wildhunt Benefit:** While shifted, you have **Advantage on all Wisdom checks** (Perception, Survival, Insight, Medicine), AND **no creature within 30 feet of you can make an attack roll with Advantage against you** unless you are incapacitated. *(This completely shuts down enemy Pack Tactics, Reckless Attack, invisible stalkers, and flanking!)*

### Rogue Class Features (Levels 1–9)
- **Expertise (Lvl 1 & 6):** Doubled proficiency bonus (+8) in *Stealth*, *Perception*, *Survival*, and *Athletics* (or *Thieves' Tools*).
- **Sneak Attack (`5d6`, Lvl 1):** Once per turn, deal an extra `5d6` damage when you hit with a finesse/ranged weapon and either have Advantage OR an ally is within 5 ft. of the target.
- **Cunning Action (Lvl 2):** Use your **Bonus Action** to **Dash**, **Disengage**, or **Hide**.
- **Steady Aim (Lvl 3, Tasha's Optional):** As a **Bonus Action**, if you haven't moved this turn, reduce your speed to 0 to give yourself **automatic Advantage** on your next attack roll this turn. *(Flavor: You crouch low on all fours, ears pinned back, locking onto the target's jugular before lunging).*
- **Uncanny Dodge (Lvl 5):** When an attacker you can see hits you with an attack, use your **Reaction** to **halve the attack's damage**. *(Flavor: Supernatural werewolf regeneration and unnatural resilience against non-silvered blows).*
- **Evasion (Lvl 7):** When subjected to a DEX save for half damage (fireballs, breath weapons, traps), you take **0 damage on a success** and only **half damage on a failure**.

### Phantom Subclass Features (Levels 3 & 9)
*Horror Movie Flavor: Just like David Kessler in **An American Werewolf in London**, the ghosts of those killed by the curse (and by your own claws) cling to you, whispering warnings and tearing at the souls of your prey.*

- **Whispers of the Dead (Lvl 3):** Whenever you finish a short or long rest, choose one skill or tool proficiency you lack—a ghostly victim shares its lingering mortal knowledge with you.
- **Wails from the Grave (Lvl 3 — 4 uses / Long Rest + Soul Trinkets):** Immediately after you deal your Sneak Attack damage to a creature on your turn, you can target a second creature you can see within 30 feet of the first creature. Roll half the number of Sneak Attack dice (**`3d6`**) and deal that much **Necrotic damage** to the second target as spectral wolves/victims howl and tear through them.
- **Tokens of the Departed (Lvl 9 Feature):**
  - **Harvest Soul (Reaction):** Whenever a creature dies within 30 feet of you, you can use your **Reaction** to snatch a part of its departing soul into a **Soul Trinket** (a bloodstained tooth, a lock of hair, a tarnished silver coin). You can hold up to **4 Soul Trinkets** at once.
  - **Passive Boost (While holding ≥1 Trinket):** You have **Advantage on Death Saving Throws** and **Constitution Saving Throws**—your cursed body refuses to die!
  - **Consume Trinket (Free on Sneak Attack):** Destroy one trinket immediately after dealing Sneak Attack to use **Wails from the Grave** *without* expending one of your daily uses!
  - **Interrogate the Dead (Action):** Destroy a trinket to force the spirit to answer one question (*Speak with Dead* style).

## Feats & Ability Score Improvements (Levels 4, 8)

With a starting rolled **16 DEX** (+2 Racial = 18), taking two half-feats brings DEX to **20**:

1. **Level 4 — Piercer (*Tasha's*):**
   - **+1 DEX** (brings DEX from 18 to 19).
   - **Rending Fangs:** Once per turn, **reroll one piercing damage die** when you hit with a piercing attack (including your `5d6` Sneak Attack dice!).
   - On a critical hit with a piercing weapon, roll **one additional weapon damage die**.
2. **Level 8 — Slasher (*Tasha's*):**
   - **+1 DEX** (maxes DEX at **20** for +5 to hit/damage/AC/Stealth/Initiative).
   - **Hamstringing Claws:** Once per turn when you hit a creature with an attack that deals slashing damage, you **reduce the target's speed by 10 feet** until the start of your next turn!
   - **Grievous Wound (Crit):** When you score a critical hit that deals slashing damage to a creature, you grievously wound it—until the start of your next turn, the target has **Disadvantage on all attack rolls**!

## Optional DM Module: "True Lycanthropy" Overlay
*If your DM wants to grant you the actual **Monster Manual Werewolf Curse** (or **Grim Hollow** transformation) on top of your Shifter Phantom Rogue, share this balanced Level 9 tweak with them:*

- **Hybrid / Wolf Transformation:** Merged into your *Shifting* feature (or at-will under the full moon).
- **Natural Weapons (Finesse Claws & Bite):** While in Hybrid or Wolf form, your unarmed Bite (`1d8`) and Claws (`2d4`) count as **Simple Finesse Melee Weapons** with which you are proficient, allowing them to trigger your `5d6` Sneak Attack without needing to hold a weapon.
- **Cursed Hide & Silver Vulnerability:** While shifted, you gain **Resistance to non-magical Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage from non-silvered weapons**, balanced by **Vulnerability to damage from Silvered weapons** (or taking `1d6` radiant burn when touched by silver/wolfsbane).
- **Bloodlust Save (Horror Flaw):** If you drop below 25% HP (18 HP) or smell fresh blood in the dark, make a DC 12 Wisdom save or you must spend your turn attacking the nearest creature—friend or foe!
