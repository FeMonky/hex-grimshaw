The Grimshaw Inheritance (Generational Wifwolf Curse)

You fled Grimshaw Hall ten years ago to become a city cat-burglar ("Hex"), trying to outrun your family's sinister reputation. Six months ago, your brother Ben was found torn to pieces on the moors. Returning for his funeral, you discovered the truth in the iron-barred cellar: a monster wasn't hunting your family—your family WAS the monster. You survived the night the manor burned, inheriting the wifwolf curse and the bickering ghosts of your bloodline.

Gallows Humor & Paranoia
  • Silver Check: Discreetly tests tavern cutlery against her thumb and asks if the stew contains wolfsbane "purely out of culinary curiosity."
  • Exit Mapping: Never sits with her back to a door; counts every window, lock, and chimney upon entering.
  • Talking to Ghosts: Mutters dry comebacks under her breath to her mangled brother Ben's ghost hovering over her shoulder.
Table Quips (Click to Roll a Random Line)
Wildhunt Shifting Bonus Action

1 min (4/LR). Gain 13 Temp HP (PB 4 + Level 9), Advantage on WIS checks, and no creature within 30 ft can make an attack roll with Advantage against you while you aren't incapacitated.

Wails from the Grave Free on Sneak Attack

After dealing Sneak Attack, deal 3d6 Necrotic to a 2nd creature within 30 ft. (4/LR, or crush a Soul Trinket).

Tokens of the Departed Reaction (30 ft)

Snatch a Soul Trinket when a creature dies within 30 ft (max 4). Having ≥1 grants Adv on Death & CON saves. Crush 1 for free Wails or 1 question to the spirit.

Piercer + Slasher + Evasion

Piercer (Bite): Reroll 1 piercing die/turn; +1 weapon die on Crit.
Slasher (Claws): Reduce hit target's speed by 10 ft/turn; Crit gives target Disadvantage on all attacks until your next turn!
Uncanny Dodge & Evasion: Reaction halve attack dmg; 0 dmg on passed DEX save.
(Note: Next ASI unlocks at Level 10 — Sentinel recommended).

Optional True Lycanthropy Overlay

If your DM enables full Dread Domain / Monster Manual Lycanthropy:

  • Finesse Natural Weapons: Unarmed Bite (1d8) and Claws (2d4) count as Finesse weapons eligible for Sneak Attack.
  • Cursed Hide: Resistance to non-magical, non-silvered Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing damage; Vulnerability to Silvered weapons.
  • Harkon's Bite (Uncommon Item): Wolf-tooth talisman granting +1 to all Saves and Checks.
